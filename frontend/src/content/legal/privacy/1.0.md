This Privacy Policy explains how Global Product Identification Inc., a Canadian federal corporation (“Global Product Identification,” “Identification ID,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, discloses, retains, and protects personal information in connection with the Identification ID platform, website, manufacturer registry, product registry, APIs, communications, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy applies to users of Identification ID, including manufacturer representatives, account holders, website visitors, API users, business contacts, and other individuals whose personal information we process in connection with the Services.

Global Product Identification Inc. is responsible for personal information under its control.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Identification ID, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 1.1 Account Information

When you create or use an Identification ID account, we may collect:

- name;
- email address;
- account credentials and authentication information;
- company name;
- job title or business role;
- telephone number, where provided;
- account preferences;
- account status;
- registration date;
- verification status; and
- other information provided during account creation or management.

We recommend that users do not provide unnecessary personal information in public company or product fields.

## 2. Manufacturer and Business Information

Manufacturers may provide information including:

- legal or trading name;
- business address;
- country or jurisdiction;
- company website;
- corporate email address;
- telephone number;
- company description;
- brands;
- logo;
- product information;
- manufacturer registration information;
- Manufacturer ID (“MID”);
- verification-related information;
- and other business information.

Some manufacturer information is intended to be publicly displayed as part of the Identification ID registry.

Business information that does not identify an individual may not constitute personal information under applicable privacy law. However, where business information identifies an individual, we handle it in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## 3. Product Information and Content

Manufacturers may submit product-related information such as:

- product names;
- models;
- descriptions;
- specifications;
- images;
- Product Identification IDs (“IIDs”);
- QR codes;
- manuals;
- PDF documents;
- warranty information;
- technical documentation;
- safety information;
- certification information;
- product URLs; and
- other product data.

Product information is generally intended for business or public registry purposes and may be displayed publicly.

Manufacturers are responsible for ensuring that they have authority to submit personal information contained in documents or other content uploaded to Identification ID.

## 4. Payment Information

When you purchase a Manufacturer Registry Membership or another paid Service, payment transactions may be processed by a third-party payment processor.

Identification ID may receive transaction-related information such as:

- customer or account identifier;
- subscription status;
- plan;
- billing interval;
- payment status;
- transaction amount;
- currency;
- invoice information;
- limited payment-method information; and
- billing contact information.

Where payment-card information is entered directly into a payment processor's systems, Identification ID does not need to receive or store the complete payment-card number.

Payment processing is also subject to the payment provider's privacy practices.

## 5. Technical and Security Information

When you use the Services, we may automatically collect technical information including:

- IP address;
- browser type;
- device type;
- operating system;
- date and time of requests;
- pages or features accessed;
- login activity;
- session information;
- security events;
- API activity;
- request and error logs;
- QR and Product ID lookup activity;
- approximate location derived from IP address where applicable; and
- other information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the Services.

We may use this information for fraud prevention, cybersecurity, troubleshooting, analytics, registry integrity, and service improvement.

## 6. QR, Product Lookup and Analytics Data

Identification ID may collect information when users scan QR codes, access digital product passports, search the registry, or perform Product ID or Manufacturer ID lookups.

Depending on the functionality used, this may include:

- date and time;
- requested Product ID or Manufacturer ID;
- referring page;
- device/browser information;
- IP address;
- approximate geographic information derived from IP address;
- and interaction or usage information.

We may provide aggregated or appropriately limited analytics to participating manufacturers.

We do not intend manufacturer analytics to provide manufacturers with unnecessary identifying information about individual consumers.

## 7. API Information

When users or organizations access Identification ID through an API, we may process:

- account and organization information;
- API credentials or identifiers;
- request timestamps;
- endpoints accessed;
- request volume;
- IP addresses;
- security information;
- error information;
- and data necessary to authenticate, meter, secure, and provide API access.

API secrets should not be publicly disclosed.

## 8. Communications

- name;

- company;

- Manufacturer ID;
- country;
- website;

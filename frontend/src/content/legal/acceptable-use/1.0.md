This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) governs use of the Identification ID platform, website, Manufacturer Registry, Product Registry, Manufacturer IDs (“MIDs”), Product Identification IDs (“IIDs”), QR codes, digital product passports, APIs, documents, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

The Services are operated by Global Product Identification Inc., a Canadian federal corporation (“Identification ID,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).

This AUP forms part of the Identification ID Terms of Service and Manufacturer Agreement.

By using the Services, you agree to comply with this AUP.

## 1. Purpose

Identification ID is designed to provide digital identity and registry infrastructure for legitimate manufacturers, businesses, brands, and products.

Users must use the Services honestly, lawfully, securely, and in a manner consistent with the integrity of the Identification ID registry.

## 2. Accurate Identity

You must provide accurate information about the company or organization you represent.

You must not:

- impersonate another manufacturer, company, organization, or person;
- falsely claim to represent another business or brand;
- register a Manufacturer profile without appropriate authority;
- knowingly provide false company information;
- attempt to obtain multiple Manufacturer IDs for the same entity for deceptive purposes;
- create accounts intended to mislead users about the identity of a Manufacturer.

Identification ID may investigate duplicate, conflicting, misleading, or suspicious Manufacturer registrations.

## 3. Product Registration

You may register only products that you are authorized to represent on the Platform.

You must not knowingly:

- register counterfeit products;
- register fictitious products as genuine commercial products;
- register another company's products as your own;
- misrepresent the manufacturer, brand, model, origin, specifications, or status of a product;
- duplicate Product IDs in order to mislead users;
- associate a Product ID with an unrelated physical product;
- use a Product ID in a manner intended to create a false impression of authenticity, certification, or regulatory approval.

The Manufacturer remains responsible for determining whether it has the right to register and represent each product.

## 4. Product Information

Information submitted to Identification ID must not knowingly be false, fraudulent, deceptive, or materially misleading.

This includes:

- product descriptions;
- specifications;
- model information;
- manufacturer information;
- country information;
- images;
- manuals;
- safety information;
- warranty information;
- certification information;
- regulatory claims;
- environmental claims;
- product origin claims; and
- other information made available through the Platform.

Manufacturers are responsible for maintaining information that reasonably requires updating.

## 5. Documents and Files

You may upload only files that you have the right to use and distribute.

You must not upload:

- malware;
- malicious scripts;
- executable payloads intended to compromise systems;
- files designed to deceive users;
- unlawful content;
- content that knowingly infringes third-party rights;
- fraudulent certificates;
- intentionally falsified regulatory documents;
- documents falsely presented as originating from a government, certification body, laboratory, manufacturer, or other organization.

Identification ID may restrict, quarantine, remove, or investigate suspicious files.

## 6. Intellectual Property

You must respect intellectual property rights.

You must not knowingly use the Services to:

- infringe copyright;
- misuse trademarks;
- impersonate brand owners;
- distribute unauthorized copyrighted materials;
- use logos without authorization;
- publish trade secrets belonging to another party;
- falsely claim ownership of intellectual property.

Identification ID may restrict content or accounts while investigating credible intellectual-property complaints.

## 7. Manufacturer ID and Product ID Misuse

MIDs and IIDs are identifiers within the Identification ID registry.

You must not:

- sell or transfer an MID independently of the underlying Manufacturer entity without authorization from Identification ID;
- alter or manipulate an identifier to impersonate another registry entry;
- intentionally associate an MID with an unrelated company;
- intentionally associate an IID with an unrelated product;
- create misleading copies of Identification ID registry pages;
- falsely claim that an MID or IID was issued by a government authority;
- use an MID or IID to imply regulatory approval where none exists.

Identification ID may preserve retired or inactive identifiers to protect registry integrity.

## 8. QR Code Misuse

Identification ID QR codes may be used to connect physical or digital materials to Identification ID records.

You must not knowingly:

- place a product QR code on an unrelated product;
- use another Manufacturer's QR code;
- manipulate a QR destination to mislead consumers;
- reproduce Identification ID QR codes for fraudulent purposes;
- use QR functionality to distribute malware or malicious links;
- represent a QR code as government certification or regulatory approval.

## 9. Verification Misuse

You must not misrepresent the meaning of an Identification ID verification status.

A Manufacturer must not state or imply that:

- Identification ID guarantees its products;
- Identification ID certifies product safety;
- Identification ID provides government certification;
- Identification ID has approved a product for sale;
- Identification ID guarantees product quality;
- Identification ID guarantees regulatory compliance;
- Identification ID guarantees every physical product bearing an Identifier is authentic;
- unless Identification ID expressly provides such a service and authorizes that specific representation in writing.

Use of verification badges must comply with the Identification ID Verification Policy.

## 10. Illegal and Harmful Activity

The Services must not be used to facilitate fraud or other unlawful activity.

Identification ID may restrict use involving products or content that violate applicable law.

We may require additional review for regulated or higher-risk product categories.

Registration in Identification ID does not establish that a product is legal to manufacture, import, export, distribute, advertise, or sell in any particular jurisdiction.

## 11. Regulated Products

Certain products may be subject to special laws, licensing, certification, registration, labeling, or safety requirements.

These may include, depending on jurisdiction:

- medical products;
- pharmaceuticals;
- food;
- children's products;
- automotive safety components;
- electrical equipment;
- chemicals;
- hazardous materials;
- controlled goods; and
- other regulated products.

Identification ID may require additional information, limit functionality, decline registration, or suspend publication for certain categories.

The Manufacturer remains solely responsible for applicable regulatory compliance.

## 12. Platform Security

You must not:

- attempt unauthorized access to accounts or systems;
- probe or exploit vulnerabilities without authorization;
- bypass authentication;
- circumvent access controls;
- interfere with security mechanisms;
- distribute malicious software;
- intentionally overload the Platform;
- circumvent rate limits;
- interfere with other users;
- attempt to obtain passwords, API secrets, or other credentials belonging to another user.

Good-faith security research must be conducted only under any security disclosure policy expressly provided by Identification ID.

## 13. API Use

API users must comply with applicable API terms, authentication requirements, rate limits, and security requirements.

API credentials must be protected against unauthorized use.

You must not use the API to:

- circumvent Platform restrictions;
- obtain unauthorized access to non-public information;
- intentionally overload the Services;
- reconstruct restricted databases;
- impersonate another API customer; or
- violate applicable privacy or intellectual-property laws.

## 14. Scraping and Automated Access

Automated access to public registry information must comply with applicable Platform rules and API terms.

Identification ID may restrict automated extraction that:

- materially affects Platform availability;
- bypasses technical restrictions;
- attempts to obtain non-public data;
- facilitates fraud;
- violates applicable law; or
- substantially reproduces protected portions of the Identification ID database without authorization.

## 15. Spam and Abuse

Users must not use Identification ID functionality to distribute unlawful spam, phishing messages, malware, deceptive communications, or unsolicited communications prohibited by applicable law.

Users must respect opt-out and unsubscribe requests where applicable.

## 16. Privacy

Users must not publish unnecessary personal information through public Manufacturer or Product fields.

Users may provide personal information through the Services only where they have appropriate authority or another lawful basis to do so.

Identification ID may remove or restrict personal information that creates an unreasonable privacy or security risk.

## 17. Fraud and Manipulation

Users must not manipulate Platform functionality, analytics, registry records, verification systems, QR scans, API traffic, or other metrics for fraudulent or deceptive purposes.

Examples include:

- artificial QR scans;
- fake traffic;
- automated activity intended to inflate statistics;
- fraudulent account creation;
- manipulation of verification;
- attempts to bypass subscription limits.

## 18. Investigations

Identification ID may investigate suspected violations of this AUP.

Where reasonably necessary, we may request information from the account holder concerning:

- company identity;
- authorization to represent a company or brand;
- product ownership or representation;
- uploaded content;
- suspected fraud;
- security incidents; or
- other registry-integrity concerns.

Failure to reasonably cooperate may result in restrictions where necessary to protect the Platform or third parties.

## 19. Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, Identification ID may:

- issue a warning;
- request correction;
- restrict publication;
- remove content;
- temporarily suspend functionality;
- suspend verification status;
- deactivate a Manufacturer profile;
- restrict a Product ID;
- suspend API access;
- suspend or terminate an account;
- preserve relevant evidence;
- report suspected unlawful activity where required or appropriate.

Enforcement decisions may consider severity, intent, recurrence, risk to users, legal obligations, and registry integrity.

## 20. Appeals and Corrections

Where appropriate, users may contact Identification ID regarding an enforcement decision.

Identification ID may request supporting evidence before restoring content, identifiers, profiles, or verification status.

Nothing in this section requires Identification ID to restore fraudulent, unlawful, dangerous, or materially misleading content.

## 21. Changes to This Policy

Identification ID may update this AUP as the Platform, risks, regulations, or Services evolve.

The current version and effective date will appear at the beginning of this Policy.

Material changes may be communicated to affected users where appropriate.

Cancellation of Membership does not ordinarily cause a Manufacturer ID (“MID”) to be reassigned to another Manufacturer.

- historical references;

- QR references;

- dispute records; and
- identifier uniqueness.

Inactive, Archived, Membership Expired, or another appropriate registry status.

The continued existence of an identifier does not mean that a cancelled Manufacturer continues to receive paid Membership features.

This Manufacturer Verification Policy explains the meaning, scope, operation, and limitations of Manufacturer verification provided through Identification ID, operated by Global Product Identification Inc., a Canadian federal corporation.

This Policy forms part of the Identification ID contractual and registry framework.

## 1. Purpose of Verification

Identification ID maintains a Manufacturer verification system intended to improve the reliability and integrity of Manufacturer records in the Identification ID registry.

Verification is intended to provide users with information about whether specified Manufacturer or account attributes have satisfied Identification ID's applicable verification checks.

Verification is not a substitute for independent due diligence, government registration, regulatory approval, product testing, certification, or professional advice.

## 2. Verified Manufacturer Status

A Manufacturer may receive a Verified Manufacturer status when it satisfies the verification checks required by Identification ID for that status at the relevant time.

The specific verification process may be automated, manual, or a combination of both.

Verification criteria may evolve as Identification ID improves its systems.

A Verified Manufacturer status relates to the Manufacturer record and the attributes actually checked.

It does not automatically verify every statement, product, document, employee, facility, certification, or physical item associated with the Manufacturer.

## 3. Automated Verification

Identification ID may use automated verification methods during or following Manufacturer registration.

Depending on the functionality available at the time, automated checks may evaluate information such as:

- account information;
- email verification;
- email-domain characteristics;
- company website information;
- consistency of submitted Manufacturer information;
- account ownership signals;
- duplicate Manufacturer records;
- suspicious registration patterns;
- technical or security signals; and
- other indicators designed to support registry integrity.

Passing automated verification means only that the Manufacturer satisfied the applicable automated checks.

It does not mean that Global Product Identification Inc. conducted a comprehensive legal, financial, regulatory, operational, or physical investigation of the Manufacturer.

## 4. Manual or Enhanced Review

Identification ID may conduct additional review when:

- automated checks are inconclusive;
- conflicting Manufacturer records exist;
- impersonation is suspected;
- a brand ownership dispute arises;
- suspicious activity is detected;
- a complaint is received;
- the Manufacturer operates in a higher-risk or regulated category;
- additional verification is necessary to protect registry integrity.

Identification ID may request supporting information where reasonably necessary.

## 5. Possible Verification Attributes

Where supported by the Platform, Identification ID may distinguish between individual verification attributes.

Examples may include:

**Email Verified**

The account has demonstrated control of the relevant email address.

**Domain Verified**

The applicable verification process has demonstrated control of, or another approved relationship with, a domain associated with the Manufacturer.

**Manufacturer Profile Verified**

Specified Manufacturer profile information has passed applicable Identification ID verification checks.

**Business Identity Reviewed**

Specified information concerning the business entity has undergone additional review.

**Brand Relationship Reviewed**

Specified information concerning a Manufacturer's relationship to a brand has undergone additional review.

Identification ID may add, modify, rename, or discontinue verification attributes.

A status should not be interpreted more broadly than the description provided for that status.

## 6. What Verified Manufacturer Does Not Mean

A Verified Manufacturer status does NOT, by itself, mean that:

- the Manufacturer is certified by the Government of Canada or another government;
- the Manufacturer is approved by a regulator;
- Identification ID has audited the Manufacturer;
- Identification ID guarantees the Manufacturer's financial condition;
- Identification ID guarantees the Manufacturer's legal compliance;
- Identification ID certifies its factories or facilities;
- Identification ID certifies product safety;
- Identification ID guarantees product quality;
- Identification ID guarantees product performance;
- Identification ID confirms all intellectual-property rights;
- Identification ID confirms every product is genuine;
- Identification ID guarantees that a particular physical product is authentic;
- Identification ID endorses the Manufacturer;
- Identification ID recommends purchasing the Manufacturer's products.

Verification should not be represented as any of the above.

## 7. Product Verification

Manufacturer verification and Product registration are separate concepts.

The fact that a Manufacturer is Verified does not mean that every Product ID associated with that Manufacturer has independently undergone product verification.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, a Product ID indicates that a product record exists in the Identification ID registry.

It does not constitute:

- product certification;
- product-safety approval;
- government registration;
- laboratory testing;
- regulatory authorization;
- warranty;
- proof that every physical unit is genuine.

## 8. Authenticity and Anti-Counterfeit Functionality

Identification ID may provide tools intended to support product identity, product information access, traceability, or detection of suspicious activity.

Unless a specific service expressly states otherwise, Identification ID does not guarantee the authenticity of any physical product merely because:

- it bears a QR code;
- it displays a Product ID;
- it appears in the registry;
- its Manufacturer has Verified status.

A counterfeit product may copy visible identifiers or QR codes.

Users should therefore not interpret a registry lookup alone as an unconditional guarantee of physical-product authenticity.

## 9. Manufacturer Responsibilities

Verified Manufacturers remain responsible for:

- accuracy of company information;
- accuracy of Product information;
- maintaining current information;
- regulatory compliance;
- product safety;
- product quality;
- product labeling;
- warranties;
- product claims;
- uploaded documents;
- intellectual-property rights;
- legal authority to represent brands and products.

Verification does not transfer these responsibilities to Global Product Identification Inc.

## 10. Verification Badge

Identification ID may display a verification badge or status indicator on eligible Manufacturer profiles.

The badge remains property of Global Product Identification Inc.

Manufacturers may use an authorized badge only:

- while the applicable verification status remains active;
- in the form provided or approved by Identification ID;
- without materially modifying its appearance;
- without using it in a misleading context;
- without suggesting government approval or product certification.

Identification ID may require a Manufacturer to discontinue badge use if verification expires, is suspended, or is revoked.

## 11. Free Verification

Identification ID may provide Manufacturer verification without a separate verification fee.

The fact that verification is free does not change its defined scope.

Payment for a Manufacturer Registry Membership does not automatically purchase, guarantee, or influence a positive verification outcome except where the Platform expressly defines membership activation as one of several registration requirements.

Verification decisions should be based on applicable verification criteria rather than payment for a positive status.

## 12. Verification and Manufacturer Registry Membership

Manufacturer Registry Membership and Manufacturer verification are separate concepts.

A Manufacturer may pay for registry services without receiving or retaining a particular verification status if verification requirements are not satisfied.

Similarly, Identification ID may provide certain verification functions without charging a separate verification fee.

Payment does not constitute certification.

## 13. Reverification

Identification ID may require reverification when reasonably necessary.

Possible reasons include:

- material company-information changes;
- change of domain;
- change of account control;
- suspicious activity;
- conflicting registry information;
- extended inactivity;
- security concerns;
- ownership changes;
- complaints;
- changes to verification standards.

Verification status may be temporarily restricted while reverification is pending.

## 14. Suspension or Revocation

Identification ID may suspend, modify, or revoke verification where reasonably necessary, including where:

- verification was granted based on incorrect information;
- information later becomes materially inaccurate;
- impersonation is detected;
- account compromise is suspected;
- fraudulent activity is detected;
- the Manufacturer materially violates Platform rules;
- a legitimate ownership or authorization dispute exists;
- continued display of the status would materially mislead users.

Revocation of verification does not necessarily result in deletion of the Manufacturer ID.

## 15. Registry History

Identification ID may maintain internal records concerning:

- verification events;
- verification dates;
- verification methods or categories;
- status changes;
- suspensions;
- reverification;
- complaints;
- relevant supporting evidence;
- administrative decisions.

Such records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, registry integrity, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

## 16. Public Display

Where appropriate, Identification ID may publicly display:

- Manufacturer name;
- Manufacturer ID;
- current verification status;
- verification date;
- verification attributes;
- status changes;
- other information necessary to explain the current registry status.

Identification ID is not required to publicly disclose confidential verification methods, fraud-detection rules, security signals, or sensitive supporting documentation.

## 17. No Endorsement

Verification does not constitute an endorsement, recommendation, partnership, agency relationship, sponsorship, or guarantee by Global Product Identification Inc.

Manufacturers must not represent otherwise.

## 18. Errors and Corrections

Automated systems may occasionally produce incorrect, incomplete, or outdated results.

Identification ID may correct a verification status when an error is discovered.

Manufacturers may contact Identification ID to request review of a verification issue.

Identification ID may require supporting evidence before changing a status.

## 19. Third-Party Reliance

Users, consumers, distributors, retailers, marketplaces, API customers, and other third parties should consider Verification as one information signal rather than a substitute for their own due diligence.

Third parties remain responsible for determining what checks are appropriate for their particular transaction or use case.

## 20. Changes to Verification Standards

Identification ID may improve or modify verification methods as technology, fraud patterns, registry requirements, and applicable laws evolve.

A Manufacturer that satisfied an earlier verification standard may be required to complete additional verification under a newer standard.

Material changes affecting existing Manufacturers may be communicated where appropriate.

## 21. Contact and Disputes

Manufacturers may contact Identification ID regarding verification status, suspected impersonation, duplicate Manufacturer records, or verification errors.

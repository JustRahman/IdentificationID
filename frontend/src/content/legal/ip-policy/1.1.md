Identification ID respects intellectual property rights and expects Manufacturers and other users of the Platform to do the same.

This Intellectual Property & Trademark Complaint Policy describes how Global Product Identification Inc., a Canadian federal corporation and operator of Identification ID (“Identification ID,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles reports concerning alleged infringement, unauthorized brand representation, impersonation, and related disputes.

This Policy should be read together with the Identification ID Terms of Service and Manufacturer Agreement and Acceptable Use Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Policy may apply to complaints involving:

- trademarks;
- logos;
- copyrighted images;
- photographs;
- product manuals;
- PDF documents;
- marketing materials;
- product descriptions;
- company names;
- brand names;
- Manufacturer profiles;
- Product profiles;
- suspected company or brand impersonation;
- unauthorized registration of products;
- and other intellectual-property-related content appearing through Identification ID.

## 2. Manufacturer Responsibility

Manufacturers are responsible for ensuring that they have sufficient rights or authorization to upload, publish, display, or otherwise use content through Identification ID.

Registration of a Manufacturer, brand, or product with Identification ID does not independently establish legal ownership of that company name, brand, trademark, copyright, design, patent, or other intellectual property.

Similarly, issuance of an MID or IID does not determine legal ownership of intellectual property.

## 3. Trademark Complaints

A trademark owner or authorized representative may submit a complaint if they believe Identification ID is being used to:

- impersonate their brand;
- display their trademark without authorization;
- falsely claim affiliation with their company;
- register an unauthorized Manufacturer profile;
- associate their trademark with unrelated products;
- otherwise use their trademark in a materially misleading manner.

Identification ID may request evidence of trademark rights and authority to act for the rights holder.

## 4. Copyright Complaints

A copyright owner or authorized representative may report content they reasonably believe infringes their copyright.

Examples may include:

- product photographs;
- manuals;
- diagrams;
- written materials;
- videos;
- graphics;
- downloadable documents; or
- other protected works.

Identification ID may request information sufficient to identify the work and the allegedly infringing material.

## 5. Brand or Manufacturer Impersonation

A company may report a Manufacturer profile that falsely claims to represent that company or one of its brands.

Identification ID may temporarily restrict certain profile functionality while investigating a credible impersonation complaint where reasonably necessary to prevent user confusion or harm.

## 6. Information Required for a Complaint

To allow Identification ID to investigate efficiently, a complaint should generally include:

- complainant's full name;
- company or organization, if applicable;
- contact email;
- explanation of the complainant's rights or authority;
- identification of the intellectual property involved;
- Identification ID URL, MID, IID, profile, document, or content being reported;
- description of the alleged infringement or impersonation;
- supporting evidence reasonably available to the complainant;
- a statement that the information submitted is accurate to the complainant's knowledge; and
- electronic or physical signature of the complainant or authorized representative where appropriate.

Identification ID may request additional information where necessary.

## 8. Initial Review

Upon receiving a sufficiently detailed complaint, Identification ID may:

- review the reported content;
- review relevant registry records;
- request additional information;
- contact the affected Manufacturer;
- temporarily restrict content;
- temporarily suspend a verification badge;
- restrict a Manufacturer or Product profile;
- preserve relevant records;
- take no immediate action where the complaint is insufficiently supported.

The appropriate response depends on the circumstances.

## 9. Notice to the Manufacturer

Where appropriate and legally permitted, Identification ID may provide the affected Manufacturer with information concerning the complaint and an opportunity to respond.

The Manufacturer may be asked to provide evidence concerning:

- company identity;
- brand authorization;
- trademark rights;
- copyright permissions;
- distributor or licensing relationships;
- authorization to represent a product;
- ownership or source of uploaded content.

## 10. Temporary Restrictions

Identification ID may temporarily restrict disputed content while a complaint is reviewed where reasonably necessary to reduce potential harm or confusion.

Temporary restriction does not constitute a final determination that infringement occurred.

## 11. Possible Outcomes

Following review, Identification ID may:

- leave the content unchanged;
- request modification;
- remove particular content;
- restrict public display;
- restore previously restricted content;
- modify or suspend verification status;
- mark a registry record as disputed;
- deactivate a Manufacturer or Product profile;
- suspend or terminate an account for serious or repeated violations;
- take another proportionate action.

Identification ID is not a court and does not generally determine ultimate ownership of disputed intellectual property.

Complex ownership disputes may need to be resolved between the parties or by an appropriate legal authority.

## 12. Manufacturer Response

A Manufacturer that believes content was incorrectly restricted may provide a response explaining its authority to use the disputed material.

Supporting information may include:

- trademark registrations;
- licences;
- authorization letters;
- copyright ownership information;
- distribution agreements;
- brand-owner authorization;
- corporate records;
- other relevant evidence.

Identification ID may provide relevant portions of the response to the complainant where appropriate and legally permitted.

## 13. False or Abusive Complaints

Complaints must be submitted in good faith.

Identification ID may reject or take appropriate action regarding complaints that appear intentionally false, fraudulent, abusive, or submitted primarily to interfere with a competitor.

Submitting a complaint does not guarantee removal of content.

## 14. Repeat Infringement

Identification ID may suspend or terminate users that repeatedly or seriously violate intellectual property rights or repeatedly impersonate companies or brands.

The appropriate action will depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

## 15. Manufacturer ID Disputes

An MID identifies a Manufacturer record within Identification ID and does not independently establish ownership of a company name or trademark.

If multiple parties claim authority over the same Manufacturer identity, Identification ID may:

- temporarily restrict changes;
- request evidence from both parties;
- suspend verification;
- mark the record as under review;
- preserve the existing MID pending resolution.

Identification ID should seek to preserve identifier continuity where reasonably possible.

## 16. Product ID Disputes

An IID identifies a product record within Identification ID.

A Product ID does not independently determine:

- patent ownership;
- trademark ownership;
- copyright ownership;
- regulatory approval;
- distribution rights;
- physical-product ownership.

Where Product representation is disputed, Identification ID may restrict or annotate the relevant registry record while investigating.

## 17. Transfer of Company or Brand Ownership

Corporate acquisitions, mergers, trademark assignments, restructurings, or brand sales may affect control of a Manufacturer profile.

Identification ID may require evidence before transferring administrative control of an existing Manufacturer record.

Where appropriate, the existing MID should remain associated with the continuing Manufacturer identity rather than creating unnecessary duplicate identifiers.

## 18. Historical Records

Removal or restriction of disputed content does not necessarily require deletion of all internal historical records.

Identification ID may preserve information necessary for:

- registry integrity;
- fraud prevention;
- dispute resolution;
- audit history;
- legal compliance;
- security; and
- enforcement of Platform policies.

## 19. Search Engines and Third-Party Copies

Content previously made public through Identification ID may have been indexed, cached, copied, or archived by independent third parties.

Removal from Identification ID does not guarantee immediate removal from third-party search engines, archives, websites, applications, or other systems outside Identification ID's control.

## 20. No Legal Determination

Identification ID's decision to restrict, remove, restore, or leave content available is an administrative Platform decision.

It does not constitute a judicial determination concerning intellectual-property ownership or infringement.

Identification ID may comply with binding court orders or other legally valid requirements applicable to it.

## 21. Legal Requests

Where a dispute is the subject of litigation or a formal legal proceeding, Identification ID may preserve relevant records and respond to lawful requests as required by applicable law.

Nothing in this Policy requires Identification ID to disclose confidential or personal information without an appropriate legal basis.

## 22. Changes to This Policy

Identification ID may update this Policy as the Platform, registry, legal requirements, and complaint procedures evolve.

The current version and effective date will appear at the beginning of this Policy.
